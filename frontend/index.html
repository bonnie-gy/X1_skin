01 / PRODUCT SYSTEM

From flexible sensing to spatial haptics, a complete interaction loop

Our roadmap prioritizes engineering feasibility: validate the system and market with proven components first, then introduce advanced flexible sensing materials such as MXene/CNT, LIG, and ZnO.

CURRENT PREORDER PRODUCT

X1 Skin
Proof of Concept PRODUCT 01

FLEXIBLE SENSOR LAYER

X1 Skin

A flexible e-skin that conforms to body contours, capturing pressure, stretch, and posture changes as real-time input for haptic feedback.

Distributed sensing

Pressure, stretch, and posture data are mapped by spatial position, with raw-data export and independent calibration.

Chained haptic authoring

Encode collision, direction, and movement events into locatable, propagating, repeatable haptic patterns on the body.

Modular engineering architecture

The electronics layer is removable, haptic modules are replaceable, and the skin-contact layer is washable for frequent B2B deployment and maintenance.
